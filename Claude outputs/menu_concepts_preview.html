B · The Playbill. A vintage theatre broadside on the board's dark table. The ten modes become a three-act bill (Play / Campaign / Study), and the act tabs reuse the ACT I/II/III tab colours from the printed card frames. A soft stage light follows the pointer; a fleuron slides in beside the hovered billing. Selecting closes the curtain (0.7 s) — the same red as the Act III card. The footer line can rotate a random "tonight" match-up from your decks. Phone: the acts stack.
