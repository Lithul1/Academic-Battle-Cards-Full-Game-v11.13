3 · Boss reveal & the battle table

Bosses keep their satire (the Algorithm Cores) but are introduced like a monster from a bestiary: a gilded arch, candlelight and their rule printed as a curse. Battles in a run get a tabletop skin: dark wood, candle glow and a parchment board. It's the same board, layout and controls as everywhere else, re-dressed only in Scrimmage.
